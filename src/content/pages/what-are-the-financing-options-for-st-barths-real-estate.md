## Types of Mortgages Available

While cash purchases dominate the market, **mortgages** can be a practical route for many buyers. The two primary types of mortgages available in St Barths are **capital repayment** and **interest-only mortgages**. For clients with significant liquid assets, interest-only mortgages can be a promising option, as they require lower monthly payments while allowing buyers to invest the capital elsewhere.

### Capital Repayment Mortgages

With **capital repayment mortgages**, homeowners pay off both the principal and interest over time. This type of loan ensures that the mortgage is cleared by the end of the term. It's ideal for buyers looking for a long-term investment strategy and who value owning their asset outright without debt hanging over them.

### Interest-Only Mortgages

**Interest-only mortgages** allow you to pay only the interest on the property for a predetermined period. This option can provide you with increased cash flow, offering the flexibility to invest elsewhere or enjoy your luxurious home while gradually building equity. However, it’s vital to have a clear repayment strategy to avoid potential financial strain later.

## Financing for Non-Residents

Many buyers in St Barths are international investors. If you’re not a French citizen, securing a bank loan is still achievable, although banks may ask for a more significant down payment and may prefer established credit histories. Engaging with a trusted financial partner can help streamline this process, as they can guide you through the specific requirements for **foreign buyers**.
