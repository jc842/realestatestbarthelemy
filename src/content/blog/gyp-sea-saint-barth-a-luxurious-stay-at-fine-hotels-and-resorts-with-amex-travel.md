At Gyp Sea Saint Barth, you can expect nothing short of luxurious comfort. The hotel features **22 bungalows and villas**, each skillfully designed to echo the traditional architecture of the West Indies. These lodgings are not just places to rest your head; they are a *visual feast*, blending eclectic tropical design with a flair that will wow any guest. Whether nestled in the hills with breathtaking views or situated right on St Jean Beach next to the Gyp Sea Beach Club, you are assured a tranquil environment.

As you step into your room, you’ll be greeted by opulent decor, complete with elaborately crafted headboards and vibrant textiles adorned with **parrots and pineapples**. Dining at Gyp Sea is nothing short of a culinary adventure.
