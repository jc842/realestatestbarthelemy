## The Caribbean Rum Awards: A Celebration of Excellence

Every November, the stunning island of St. Barth hosts the prestigious **Caribbean Rum Awards**, inviting connoisseurs and curious newcomers alike to experience the very best in rum. This one-of-a-kind festival is dedicated to elevating the recognition of rum as a premium spirit worthy of appreciation, akin to scotch or whiskey. With multiple tastings, master classes, and an international blind-tasting competition, the Rum Awards provide an unparalleled opportunity to learn about the craft of rum distillation and the rich flavors from various regions.
