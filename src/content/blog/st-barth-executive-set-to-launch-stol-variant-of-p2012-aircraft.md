**St Barth Executive** is set to introduce the **STOL variant** of the **P2012 aircraft**, targeting short takeoff and landing capabilities. This exciting launch aims to enhance travel between the French islands of **Guadeloupe** and **Saint Barthelemy**. The aircraft will enable scheduled flights on routes that were previously only boat-accessible, significantly cutting down travel times to about **50 minutes**.

Starting mid-June, St Barth Executive plans to operate a fleet of three P2012 STOL planes, anticipating the demand to support up to **six daily flights** during peak season. This new level of accessibility is expected to boost both **tourism** and **business travel** in the region.

The modified P2012 STOL features a wider wingspan and can accommodate nine passengers, powered by two **Continental GTSIO-520-S engines**. It's tailor-made for those short inter-island trips, providing a luxurious travel experience.

Get ready for an exciting new chapter in Caribbean air travel! St Barth Executive is set to launch their **short takeoff and landing (STOL)** variant of the Tecnam P2012 aircraft. ## Introducing the P2012 STOL: A Game Changer in Caribbean Aviation

The newly designed **P2012 STOL** aircraft comes equipped with features specifically tailored to cater to the needs of inter-island travelers. With a broader wingspan compared to its predecessor, the modified aircraft is designed for improved aerodynamics and efficiency during takeoff and landing.

Imagine stepping onto an aircraft that can whisk you away from Guadeloupe to the picturesque shores of Saint Barthelemy in less than an hour! This leap in travel convenience will not only delight tourists but also become a strong asset for businesses needing quick transport in the region.

### What Makes the P2012 STOL Stand Out?

One of the most remarkable characteristics of the P2012 STOL is its **short takeoff and landing capabilities**. This allows it to service routes and destinations that larger aircraft simply can’t reach, enhancing accessibility for travelers. With a flying range perfectly suited for island hopping, this aircraft is about to redefine quite a few travel experiences in the Caribbean.

Equipped with two powerful **Continental GTSIO-520-S** engines, this aircraft holds nine passenger seats, ensuring a comfortable ride for groups or families wishing to explore the beautiful islands. The commitment by St Barth Executive to begin operations with this fleet underlines their intention to elevate air travel in the Caribbean.

## Scheduled Flights and Passenger Demand

St Barth Executive is set to kick off operations in mid-June with a fleet of three P2012 STOL aircraft.
