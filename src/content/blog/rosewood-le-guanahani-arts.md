## Embracing Creative Culture

At Rosewood Le Guanahani, the love for art isn't just appreciated; it’s celebrated. The resort has recently launched its own **Art Program**, serving as a gateway into the vibrant creative culture that St. Barth is known for. This program showcases a series of carefully curated experiences designed to immerse guests in the island's artistic community.

This initiative marks a new chapter in the hotel’s efforts to fuse **luxury living** with a profound appreciation for culture and creativity. Guests have the unique opportunity to gain privileged access to artist encounters, guided tours, and customized self-guided itineraries, all while exploring exhibitions by local artists in residence. At the heart of this program is a genuine desire to share St. Barth's artistic heritage with everyone who walks through the resort’s doors.

## An Open-Air Gallery Experience

Imagine walking through the stunning grounds of Rosewood Le Guanahani, where every corner reveals extraordinary **artworks** embedded in lush tropical surroundings. Thanks to a dynamic collaboration with the Artists of St. Barth, the resort has transformed into an **open-air art gallery**. Each piece—whether a painting, sculpture, or installation—invites guests to discover the artistry that flourishes throughout the island.

The public spaces within the hotel are constantly renewed with exhibitions featuring artists in residence, keeping the creative energy alive. Renowned fashion photographer Antoine Verglas is currently making waves at the hotel with his evocative portraits, perfectly blending the worlds of photography and celebrity culture.

### The Artistic Community of St. Barth

St. Barth has long been a haven for artists, collectors, and culture enthusiasts from across the globe. Its **art scene** is a beautiful marriage of Caribbean soul and contemporary flair, attracting creators who thrive in this idyllic setting. Premiering a collection of various talented artists, Rosewood Le Guanahani gives guests a firsthand experience of the talent that breeds in this luscious environment.

Among the featured artists, GC presents striking tufted textile works, creating a tactile and vibrant twist to traditional mural art. Meanwhile, Dvis Dformes—a former mechanic—molds engine parts into captivating three-dimensional sculptures inspired by the natural beauty of St. Barth.

Not to be overlooked, sculptor Roger Moreau adds a poetic touch with his unique heart-shaped creations made from stones collected on the island. Together, these artists build a narrative that resonates with the essence of St. Barth, infusing the resort’s atmosphere with a distinct creative soul.

## Engaging with Artists Through Events

Another exciting facet of Rosewood Le Guanahani's art program is the organization of artist-led salons, discussions, and previews that run throughout the year. These events are open to the public, providing a rare opportunity for guests and locals alike to engage with the island's creative minds.

These enlightening gatherings dive deep into the stories behind the art, showcasing the passion and dedication that each artist brings to their work. It’s not just about admiring the pieces; it’s about understanding the compelling narratives that breathe life into them.

## Guided Artistic Tours: Immersive Journeys

For those looking to delve deeper into the island's artistic pulse, the hotel offers two distinct formats of tours as part of its art program. The first is an **artistic guided tour** that provides an immersive three-hour experience led by a local expert. This journey allows guests to explore main artistic sites in Gustavia, starting from the hotel’s own impressive collection.

The tour commences with a champagne toast at the St. Barth Artists’ Gallery, setting the tone for an enriching experience. As guests stroll through Gustavia, they’re treated to a privileged view of the historic downtown area, dotted with charming architecture that beautifully melds colonial history with contemporary designs.

### Behind the Scenes: Meeting Local Artists

A highlight of the guided tour is a private meeting at artist Véronique Vandernoot’s boutique. This intimate experience grants guests a behind-the-scenes look at her creative process while allowing them to preview new works before they hit the market. Vandernoot, known for her unique handcrafted signage, has woven her artistic signature into the very fabric of St. Barth's visual identity. 

As guests wander through the vibrant streets, they’ll also catch a glimpse of the Anglican church, which stands as a symbol of the island's rich history. This leisurely stroll wraps around the artistic narrative that defines St. Barth, leaving guests enriched and informed about the culture they are immersed in.

## Self-Guided Art Tour: A Personalized Experience

For those who prefer to explore at their own pace, the **self-guided art tour** offers a flexible yet culturally enriching itinerary. Upon picking up their rental cars, guests are provided with a beautifully illustrated map curated by a local artist. This map highlights notable creative destinations, including the St. Barth Artists’ Gallery and various museums scattered throughout Gustavia.

The self-guided tour unfolds as an invitation to immerse oneself in the art scene at one’s own rhythm. Each stop allows for personal discovery and a casual exploration of the island’s artistic treasures.

### The Perfect Refreshment for Discoveries

As part of this experience, guests also receive a refreshing Le Guanahani artisanal mocktail—perfect for quenching thirst during an afternoon filled with artistic discovery. This little touch adds to the luxurious nature of the adventure while further embodying the spirit of creativity that Rosewood Le Guanahani stands for.
