Ken Griffin is spearheading a partnership to secure **air rights** from the historic **St. Bartholomew's Church** in Midtown Manhattan. This strategic move, involving a deal worth around **$78 million**, will see the church sell at least **150,000 square feet** of its development rights. The proceeds are intended to support the maintenance of the church, while the purchased air rights will facilitate the construction of a new **office tower** at **350 Park Avenue**. This ambitious project aims to reshape the skyline and draw professionals back to Manhattan.

Recently, the spotlight has shone brightly on the bustling streets of Midtown Manhattan as a major real estate deal is taking shape around the historic **St. Bartholomew’s Church**. ## The Players in the Game

When speaking about this ambitious project, it's impossible not to mention the crucial player at the helm, **Ken Griffin**. The founder of Citadel, an influential hedge fund, Griffin has often made headlines for his bold and transformative ventures across multiple markets. His partnership in this air rights acquisition includes notable entities such as **Rudin Management** and **Vornado Realty Trust**. All three are titans of the real estate industry, bringing together significant resources, expertise, and innovative visions aimed at reshaping the Manhattan skyline.

### The Importance of Air Rights

In New York City, **air rights** can be a game-changer. They represent the ability to build upward—essentially the space above a property that can be utilized to construct additional floors or, in this case, an entirely new structure. The acquisition of air rights above St. Bart’s reflects a strategic initiative not just for high-rises, but provides essential funding for the church’s maintenance, ensuring that this venerable landmark remains a community fixture.

### The Financials at Play

The financial aspect of this venture is equally compelling. The deal involves a staggering investment, with reports suggesting that the **the partnership is prepared to pay upwards of $78 million** for the rights to develop more than 150,000 square feet of airspace. These funds will play a vital role in the preservation of St. Bartholomew’s Church, which has been an iconic part of the Midtown Manhattan scenery since its establishment. This transaction showcases how real estate development can intertwine with community obligation, blending profit with purpose.

## St. Bartholomew’s Church: A Historic Landmark

Founded in 1835, St. Bartholomew’s Church stands as one of New York City's treasures. Renowned for its Gothic architecture, beautiful stained glass windows, and vibrant community, the church plays a central role not only as a place of worship but as a cultural landmark. The decision to sell its air rights also showcases a sensible approach to revenue generation amid the rising costs of maintenance and restoration of such historic edifices. The church, under Right Reverend Dean E. Wolfe’s guidance, is working closely with the developers to ensure that the financial outcomes benefit its rich history and future needs.
