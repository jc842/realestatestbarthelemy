## C.P.E. Bach: The Composer and His Legacy

Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach (C.P.E. Bach) was the second son of the legendary **Johann Sebastian Bach**, and his influence on music is undeniable. Born in **1714**, C.P.E. Bach was instrumental in transitioning music from the Baroque period to the Classical era, bridging the gap between two distinct styles. Known for his emotional depth and innovative compositions, he brought a fresh perspective to the musical landscape.

One of the highlights of this concert will be the U.S. premiere of his oratorio, **Die Auferstehung und Himmelfahrt Jesu** (The Resurrection and Ascension of Jesus), composed between **1774** and **1778**. This exciting performance will illuminate the unique characteristics of the Rococo period, including fluid dynamics and spontaneous changes in emotion, capturing the essence of C.P.E. Bach's style. It’s rare to hear oratorios like this, making this concert even more special!
