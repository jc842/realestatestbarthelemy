Get ready to dive into the heartwarming traditions that happen at St. Bartholomew’s Parish Center during the Thanksgiving season. This year's Thanksgiving Luncheon promises an exciting day filled with delicious food, engaging activities, and a sense of community. From a savory catered meal to live performances by local talent, the event celebrates togetherness and gratitude, showcasing how the parish community comes together to create lasting memories.

## The Heart of the Community: St. Bartholomew’s Parish Center

Nestled in the charming town of Norwich, the **St. Bartholomew’s Parish Center** stands as a beacon of community spirit. This gathering place is more than just a venue; it's a hub where locals come together for various events, celebrations, and charitable initiatives. This Thanksgiving, the parish center is set to play host to a delightful luncheon aimed at bringing together folks from all walks of life. From young families to senior citizens, everyone is invited to join in the festivities.

## A Tasty Tradition: The Thanksgiving Meal

One of the highlights of the Thanksgiving Luncheon is undoubtedly the mouthwatering meal that will be served. Attendees are in for a real treat as the feast is catered by none other than **Dickey’s BBQ**. Known for their signature flavors and hearty dishes, guests can expect to enjoy some of the best BBQ around. Imagine sinking your teeth into tender turkey, savory sides, and maybe even a slice of pie to wrap up the culinary experience!

### What’s on the Menu?

The menu is designed to deliver the quintessential Thanksgiving experience. From classic roasted turkey to creamy mashed potatoes, every bite promises a taste of home. Plus, with a selection of festive sides, there’s something for everyone. The warm ambiance of the parish center will ensure you feel right at home as you share this meal with old friends and new.

## Entertainment that Engages

As if the meal isn't exciting enough, the Thanksgiving Luncheon at St. Bartholomew's also features **live music** provided by the talented Norwich High School Music Department. This addition really elevates the atmosphere, allowing guests to sit back, relax, and enjoy some tunes as they feast. Whether it’s traditional holiday songs or festive pop tunes, the music adds a layer of joy to the occasion.

### Community Participation

The thought behind this event emphasizes the importance of **community involvement**. Attendees are encouraged to bring a free-will donation. This ensures that the spirit of giving is upheld and that resources can continue to be provided to those in need within the local area. It’s a meaningful way to express gratitude while lending a helping hand.

## An Invitation to All

The **Thanksgiving Luncheon** is open to the entire community, creating an opportunity for everyone to come together and celebrate. Whether you are a long-time resident or new to the area, this event serves as a welcome introduction to the warmth and support that the St. Bartholomew’s community embodies.

### Details You Won’t Want to Miss

The event is set for **November 18th**, running from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the St. Bartholomew’s Parish Center located at 81 East Main Street in Norwich. Mark your calendars! This is a chance not just to enjoy a great meal, but also to connect with others.

## A Continued Legacy: Free Community Meal

Following the Thanksgiving Luncheon, the night will continue with a special tradition. On the evening of the luncheon, there will be a **free dinner** provided at St. Bart's parish center. This is a continuation of the Community Kitchen's mission to serve the community by offering free meals on Tuesdays, ensuring that no one goes hungry.

### Volunteering Opportunities

For those who wish to get more involved, there are opportunities to volunteer. This not only helps facilitate the event but also deepens one’s connection to the parish and the broader community. If you feel called to serve, don’t hesitate to reach out to St. Bartholomew’s office to learn how to join in.

## Spaghetti Supper Fundraiser: A Taste of Generosity

Before the Thanksgiving festivities, the community recently held its **71st annual Spaghetti Supper** fundraiser. This successful event saw volunteers whip up over 1,500 meatballs to serve, showcasing the power of community effort and spirit.

Such events play a vital role not just in fundraising, but in promoting connections among community members. They foster friendships and create bonds that last beyond individual events. It is essential that the vibrant culture of St. Bartholomew’s continues to flourish, bringing a message of hope and hospitality.

### Food Drives and Donations

In alignment with its tradition of community service, during the Thanksgiving Luncheon, attendees are encouraged to bring non-perishable food items for local food banks. These contributions go a long way in ensuring that local families have access to essential resources.

Particularly requested items include hearty soups, canned pasta, juice, and even diapers, among others. This collaboration with food banks in neighboring cities like **New Berlin**, **Unadilla**, **Norwich**, and **Guilford** reflects a sincere commitment to uplifting those who may face food insecurity.

## Making Reservations

If you're interested in attending this remarkable lunch, it's crucial to RSVP by November 12th. To register, simply visit the **Commerce Chenango** events page, click on the Thanksgiving Luncheon on the calendar, and follow the prompts to confirm your attendance.

This early registration helps organizers adequately prepare for the turnout and ensure everyone can be accommodated comfortably. Plus, it gives you peace of mind knowing that your spot is secured for this heartwarming occasion.

It reminds us of the importance of coming together, sharing blessings, and ensuring that everyone feels included.
