Get ready to experience a breathtaking musical journey as **St. Bartholomew's Church** prepares for the world premiere of "Precious Stones," a captivating piano concert composed by John-Luke Addison. Scheduled for February 16th at 4 p.m., this enchanting performance promises to transport audiences through the vibrant essence of various precious gemstones, translated into melodies and harmonies. With talented pianist Bryan Verhoye at the helm, attendees can expect an afternoon filled with emotion and artistry that beautifully intertwines the realms of classical music and visual inspiration.

## What to Expect from the Concert

The highly anticipated concert will showcase the premiere of "Precious Stones," a roughly **40-minute suite** composed by Addison. This work draws its inspiration from a trip he took to **Spain** and **Portugal**, where he immersed himself in the colors and architectural styles of the region. Music lovers are in for a treat as Verhoye performs not only the new piece but also a selection of other piano pieces, creating a rich tapestry of sound for the audience to enjoy.

This concert is being held at **St. Bartholomew’s Episcopal Church**, located at 16275 Pomerado Road in Poway, California. The church is known for its welcoming atmosphere and commitment to community engagement, making it an ideal venue for this exciting musical event.

## John-Luke Addison: The Visionary Behind 'Precious Stones'

As the **director of music and liturgy** at St. Bartholomew’s Church, John-Luke Addison holds a unique position in the realm of music composition. His efforts in creating "Precious Stones" spanned nearly three years, where he meticulously sought to convey the qualities of precious gemstones such as **diamonds**, **rubies**, **emeralds**, and **sapphires** through sound.

Addison's approach to composition is both layered and dynamic. He describes his passion for embracing multi-faceted ideas within his work, much like the classical piece "The Planets," which captures different celestial bodies through dedicated compositions. "Precious Stones" consists of seven distinct segments, each encapsulating the unique characteristics of a specific gemstone, inviting listeners to explore the emotional depths related to each one.

### Inspiration Derived from Travel

During his travels to Spain and Portugal with his wife, Michelle, Addison was deeply inspired by the stunning **colors** and architectural nuances encountered along the way. Studying the environment, he observed how various precious gemstones influenced both the aesthetic and emotional atmospheres of each locale. Addison translated these observations into music, shaping the notes and rhythms to reflect textures, colors, and emotional responses that each gemstone evokes.

He notes, "I was looking at the colors of stones in their natural habitat and was getting inspired by what type of music would match what they are like. Music and color are so related with dark and light and soft and hard elements." This reflection further illustrates the connection humans share with the beauty of nature and how it can inspire artistic expression.

## The Role of Bryan Verhoye

Adding to the excitement of the event is the seasoned pianist, **Bryan Verhoye**. Known for his brilliant performances as a solo recitalist, accompanist, and chamber musician, Verhoye is no stranger to captivating audiences. His interpretations of music span not only San Diego but the entire West Coast, making him a renowned figure in the music community.

In addition to his extensive performance background, Verhoye also engages with educational initiatives, sharing his passion for music with students and aspiring musicians alike. He holds positions at various institutions and has been involved with the **San Diego Chamber Orchestra**, **La Jolla Music Society**, and the **Mainly Mozart Festival**. His multifaceted expertise makes him the perfect choice to bring Addison's compositions to life during the concert.

### A Premiering Composition

Not only will Verhoye spotlight "Precious Stones," but he will also perform other piano pieces beforehand, seamlessly transitioning audiences into the musical experience. The premiere of this composition holds a special significance for Addison, who has expressed a mixture of excitement and nervousness about sharing his work with an audience. As he shares, "It’s always very vulnerable in any art medium, such as painting a picture and having a soiree to unveil it.”

## The Emotional Connection in Music

For Addison, composing music is not merely about the notes on a page; it’s about the emotional journey that each piece can evoke. His past works include poignant compositions such as “Good Night: A Requiem,” which served as an homage to his late father. Addison’s deep emotional investment translates into the music he creates, inviting listeners to connect with the themes he explores.

“Good Night: A Requiem” reflects his father’s love of poetry and was performed at St. Bartholomew’s Church in November 2023. This past experience of translating personal grief into musical form likely influences the depth he seeks to convey through "Precious Stones." The concert will provide a space for attendees not only to appreciate the beauty of music but also to remember and honor their loved ones.
