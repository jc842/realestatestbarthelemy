## Understanding Carl Philipp Emanuel Bach's Influence

Carl Philip Emmanuel Bach, known as C.P.E. Bach, was the fifth child and third son of the legendary composer Johann Sebastian Bach. He played a pivotal role in the transition from the Baroque to the Classical era, laying the groundwork for composers like Haydn, Mozart, and Beethoven. C.P.E. Bach is celebrated for his innovative approaches to musical form and expression, which resonate with the emotive depth found in the works of the subsequent Romantic composers.

His compositions, particularly the concertos, sonatas, and symphonies, are known for their contrasts in mood and fluid dynamics. When you listen to his works at the **Bach at St. Bart's** performance, expect to be moved by the abrupt changes in style and emotional content that he so masterfully weaves together.
