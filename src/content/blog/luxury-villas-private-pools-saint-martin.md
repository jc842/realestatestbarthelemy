## Planning Your Saint Martin Villa Stay: Travel and Practical Tips

Arriving and navigating Saint Martin is straightforward yet filled with local charm that enhances the vacation experience. The island is served primarily by Princess Juliana International Airport on the Dutch side, renowned worldwide for its dramatic plane landings over Maho Beach.

### Transportation Options

- **Taxis:** Easily accessible from airports and popular spots; tariffs are regulated though unmetered.
- **Buses:** Reliable and budget-friendly option with extensive routes around the island.
- **Rental Cars:** Preferred by many visitors to discover the island fully; driving is on the right-hand side, and international licenses are accepted.
- **Private Transfers and Concierge Arrangements:** Many luxury villas include or offer options for private chauffeured transfers.

### Seasonal and Practical Considerations

- **Best Travel Periods:** Late fall and spring offer pleasant weather with fewer crowds.
- **Entry Requirements:** No tourist visa required for many nationalities, but onward and return ticket proof is standard.
- **Currency:** The Euro is used on the French side, while the Netherlands Antillean Guilder and US Dollar circulate on the Dutch side.
