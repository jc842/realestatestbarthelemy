As you get closer to your destination, the excitement builds. Lake Merritt's shimmering waters beckon, promising a tranquil experience after an invigorating journey. Upon arrival at Lake Merritt, you’ll be greeted by an array of outdoor activities. You can even rent a boat or simply enjoy a peaceful afternoon reading under the shade of a tree.
