## The Journey of Mama Michelangelo

Mama Michelangelo, a standout restaurant that established itself in Antibes over 30 years ago, has a legacy of bringing the joys of Italian cuisine to its diners. With the recent expansion to St. Barth, it continues to delight food lovers by showcasing a menu crafted with passion. The mastermind behind this culinary delight is Chef Mamo, affectionately known as the "**Pope of Pasta and Smiles**." His dedication to authentic Italian flavors has garnered a loyal following, and now Caribbean visitors can enjoy his creations.
