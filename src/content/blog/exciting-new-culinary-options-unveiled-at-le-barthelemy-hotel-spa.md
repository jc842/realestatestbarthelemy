## New Dining Venues at Le Barthelemy Hotel & Spa

The reopening of Le Barthelemy has introduced some fantastic new dining venues that are sure to impress guests. Embracing the vibrant culinary scene of St. Barts, the hotel presents visitors with options that appeal to both traditionalists and trendsetters alike. Whether you’re in the mood for an intimate fine dining experience or a lively atmosphere filled with music and excitement, you’ll find just what you’re looking for here.

### Discover Abyss: A Fine Dining Experience

Abyss is a culinary gem at Le Barthelemy, curated by the Michelin-star executive chef Jeremy Czaplicki. This fine-dining restaurant showcases a harmonious blend of classic French techniques with the bold, radiant flavors of the Caribbean. Open every evening for dinner, guests can savor a multicourse menu that reflects the local bounty while enjoying live music in the background. It's a perfect spot for a romantic dinner or a special occasion where culinary artistry meets a sophisticated ambiance.

### Amis St. Barth: Sunday Brunch and More

If you love brunch, you're in for a treat at Amis St. Barth! This new open-air dining concept takes the essence of Caribbean casual dining and infuses it with French elegance. On Sundays, guests can dive into a lavish brunch spread, featuring live cooking stations, a boulangerie corner with freshly baked pastries, a smoothie bar, and a restorative salad bar. Beyond the fabulous brunch, Amis will also be serving up daily lunch, offering a selection of healthy, island-inspired French cuisine. From light seafood dishes to refreshing salads, you’ll find the perfect midday meal here.

## Unique Culinary Experiences to Savor

At Le Barthelemy, dining is not just about the food; it's also about the experience! The hotel has launched several innovative culinary experiences designed to enhance your gastronomic journey.

### The Four Hands Guest Chef Dinner Series

Coming soon is the highly anticipated Four Hands Guest Chef Dinner Series. This exclusive event focuses on collaboration between internationally renowned chefs and local culinary talents, creating a truly unique dining experience. Guests attending this event can expect a meticulously curated multicourse menu that showcases innovative dishes. It’s a chance to immerse yourself in an extraordinary culinary dialogue and tantalize your taste buds with flavors from both local and global kitchens.

### Seven Stars Bar: Cocktails with a Twist

The newly introduced Seven Stars Bar offers a vibrant social scene with a nod to Spanish influences. Guests can indulge in crafted cocktails that celebrate the zodiac—each drink is served in a distinctive glass, complete with a personalized leather coaster and a four-line poem that captures the essence of the sign it represents. The lively ambiance paired with live music each night makes it an ideal place to unwind after a day of adventures.

## One-of-a-Kind Rooftop Dining Experience

As part of its seasonal reopening, Le Barthelemy's rooftop bar called The Rooftop promises an incredible dining experience focused on Asian cuisine. Launching on November 30, this reinvented space will invite guests to enjoy Asian culinary delights with a spectacular view. Open on weekends from 4 to 9 p.m., it’s the perfect spot to relax and indulge in a delicious meal while soaking in the surrounding beauty of St. Barts.
