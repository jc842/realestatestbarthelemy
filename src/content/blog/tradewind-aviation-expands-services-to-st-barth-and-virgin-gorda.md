                - **Tradewind Aviation** increases flight capacity to **St Barth**.

                - New service now available for booking until **April 2025**.

                - Daily routes planned from **San Juan** to **Virgin Gorda**.

                - Regular flights between **St Thomas** and **St Barth** every Tuesday.

                - New routes to **Abaco** and **North Eleuthera** in The Bahamas launching.

            

        

This winter, **Tradewind Aviation** is ramping up its operations to some of the Caribbean's most desired luxury spots. They are adding significant capacity for flights to **St Barth** from **San Juan**, available for booking until April 2025. Additionally, they will be launching daily routes between **San Juan** and **Virgin Gorda** in the British Virgin Islands, running until April 21, with the exception of specific holidays. The airline also maintains a scheduled service from **St Thomas** to **St Barth** on Tuesdays through April 2025. Known for its upscale travel experiences, Tradewind focuses on connecting high-end travelers to exclusive destinations, solidifying its reputation as a premier choice for flights to both **St Barth** and **Virgin Gorda**.
