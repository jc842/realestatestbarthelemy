## Understanding A New Brain

A New Brain is a musical that combines the thrilling talents of **William Finn** and **James Lapine**, the extraordinary creatives behind Falsettos and The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee. This show is celebrated for its **life-affirming** message about the healing powers of love and art, making it an exciting piece for both audiences and performers alike. The show follows the story of a composer who faces a life-threatening situation, prompting him to navigate through love, creativity, and self-discovery. This layered narrative not only resonates with performers but also invites them to bring their own interpretations to the stage.

## Details of the Audition Process

The St. Bart's Players are inviting performers to step forward and take part in this ensemble show. With **open casting** available, all roles are up for grabs. This non-union, non-paid production provides a great entry point for aspiring actors and experienced performers who wish to experiment with new roles. The auditions will be held at the **St. Bart’s Players Community House** on 50th Street, offering a chance to impress the directors and score a spot in this amazing production.

### When and Where to Audition

Mark your calendars for the audition dates! The auditions will take place on **August 26th** and **August 27th**, starting at 7 PM for members and 7:30 PM for non-members. Performers are encouraged to arrive at **St. Bartholomew’s Church** to enter through the Community House at 109 East 50th Street. Be sure to arrive early, as late arrivals may not be accommodated! Callbacks will be on **August 28th**, so ensure your performance is unforgettable!

### What to Prepare for the Audition

To make a strong impression, participants need to bring specific items for the audition. A recent **headshot** is mandatory; if you don’t have one, the team will take a photo on-site. Additionally, you’ll need a copy of your **resume** and a list of **conflicts** on the audition form calendar. It’s crucial to note that having excessive conflicts may impact casting decisions. Prepare a **32-64 bar cut** of a contemporary musical theater song that aligns with the style of the show, showcasing your unique personality and acting prowess, complete with sheet music in the right key.

## Diverse and Inclusive Environment

The St. Bart's Players prioritize creating a **diverse**, **inclusive**, and **equitable** environment for all artists involved. They respect every individual's **race**, **ethnicity**, **gender identity**, **age**, **sexual orientation**, **education**, **disability**, and **physical characteristics**. This commitment to diversity allows performers to feel valued while contributing their unique perspectives to the ensemble.

## A Glimpse into the Rehearsals

Once cast, rehearsals are set to commence on **September 6th**, culminating in seven exciting performances during the production run from **September 6, 2025 - November 2, 2025**. Rehearsals will be held on **Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays** from 7 PM to 10 PM, with **Saturday sessions** from noon to 5 PM at St. Bart’s. This schedule allows performers ample time to perfect their craft leading up to the show’s premiere at THE 14Y THEATER AT 14TH STREET AND 1ST AVE, NY, NY.

### Member Dues

While you don’t need to be a member to audition, those who are cast must pay annual dues of **$75**, or just **$25** if you’re under 25. This is a small investment towards being part of a long-standing community theater that has entertained audiences since its founding in **1927**. An incredible opportunity for anyone looking to experience the vibrant theater scene in New York City!
