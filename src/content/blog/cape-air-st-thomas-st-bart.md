In an exciting development for Caribbean travel enthusiasts, Cape Air is set to launch a new service connecting St. Thomas and St. Barthelemy in the fourth quarter of 2025. This new connection signifies a step towards enhancing travel options within the Caribbean while supporting the growing tourism industry in St. Barthelemy.

The new route connecting St. Thomas and St. Barthelemy is set to operate twice daily, featuring several flights that provide flexibility for travelers. The scheduled flights include:
