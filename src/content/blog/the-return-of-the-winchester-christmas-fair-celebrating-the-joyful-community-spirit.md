                - **Annual Christmas Fair** at St Bartholomew’s Church

                - Date: **Saturday, November 30**, from **10.30am to 3.30pm**

                - Opened by **Mayor** of Winchester, Cllr Russell Gordon-Smith

                - Free **entry** with festive gifts for sale

                - **Glass engraving demonstrations** by master engraver Tracey Sheppard

                - Order **Christmas trees** and **wreaths**

                - Supporting the **Giving for Living Appeal** to restore St Bartholomew’s

            

        

The **Winchester Christmas Fair** is making a grand comeback this year, bringing together the community to celebrate the **festive spirit**! Scheduled for **November 30** from **10:30 AM to 3:30 PM**, the event will kick off with an opening by the **Mayor of Winchester**. Attendees can enjoy a range of activities, including **glass engraving demonstrations** and a new **teddy bears' picnic** for the kids. Local businesses will showcase their products, while delicious food and drinks, like **mulled wine** and **mince pies**, will be readily available. This fair not only promises fun for everyone but also aims to support the **Giving for Living Appeal**, helping to restore the iconic St Bartholomew's Church, a historic landmark.

## History of the Winchester Christmas Fair

The Winchester Christmas Fair has a rich history that dates back many years. Over time, it has evolved from a simple market to a grand festival that attracts locals and tourists alike. The event encapsulates the spirit of Christmas, transforming the charming streets of Winchester into a winter wonderland, adorned with twinkling lights and festive decorations.

Originally conceived as a way to promote local businesses and artisans, the fair quickly gained popularity. In recent years, it has expanded its range of offerings, turning into a comprehensive celebration of culture, crafts, and community. The fair not only provides a platform for vendors to showcase their goods but also fosters a sense of belonging among residents.

## A Community Affair

One of the most remarkable aspects of the Winchester Christmas Fair is the community spirit that permeates the event. Local organizations, schools, and volunteers come together to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. This collaboration fosters friendships and strengthens community bonds, making participants feel like they are part of something truly special.

### The Role of Local Businesses

The involvement of **local businesses** is pivotal to the fair's success. They provide unique products that reflect the region's culture and creativity. From handmade crafts to gourmet food items, the fair is a treasure trove of local offerings that you won't find anywhere else. It's a fantastic opportunity to support **local entrepreneurs** while finding the perfect gift for your loved ones.

### Volunteers Making It Happen

Behind the scenes, a dedicated team of volunteers works tirelessly to ensure everything runs smoothly. These unsung heroes play a significant role in organizing the event, setting up stalls, managing logistics, and creating a festive atmosphere. Their dedication and hard work shine through in the fair's success and the joy it brings to the community.

## Must-See Attractions at the Fair

Every year, the Winchester Christmas Fair rolls out attractions that enchant visitors. Here are some highlights you won’t want to miss!

### The Festive Market

The heart of the fair is undoubtedly the **festive market**, where local artisans and craftspeople gather to showcase their unique products. From beautifully crafted jewelry to exquisite home decor, there's something for everyone. Not only does this market offer a fantastic shopping experience, but it also provides a glimpse into the incredible talent of local artisans.

### Food and Drink Galore

No Christmas fair is complete without delicious food and drink options. At the Winchester Fair, you can indulge in a variety of seasonal treats. Think **mulled wine**, warm mince pies, and hearty soups that warm you up as you wander around. Food stalls serve everything from sweet treats to savory dishes, ensuring that no one goes hungry during their day of festivities.

### Live Entertainment

The fair also features **live entertainment**, including performances by local choirs and musicians. There's nothing quite like the sound of festive carols singing through the air as people gather to enjoy the holiday spirit. Families can kick back and enjoy the shows or even join in on the fun with dance parties and community sing-alongs.
