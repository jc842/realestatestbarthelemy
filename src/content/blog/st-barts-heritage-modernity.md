## The Historical Roots of St. Bart's

St. Bart's, officially known as Saint Barthélemy, has a fascinating story that dates back to its discovery by Christopher Columbus in 1493. The island was named after his brother, Bartolomeo, and has since evolved through a variety of influences. The **French** colonial past is evident in the architecture, language, and lifestyle, creating an inviting atmosphere that feels like a leap back in time.

The island was originally colonized by the French, which is why you'll find French language and culture embedded in the daily lives of the locals. This blend of history creates a backdrop for modern life, where the stunning vistas of the Caribbean meet charming villages. Traditional buildings, such as the preserved **churches** and colonial-style homes, are reminders of the island's past, and they enhance the overall vibe of St. Bart's.
