## Location and Views

Gyp Sea Hotel is perfectly positioned overlooking the breathtaking **Bay of Flamands**. Its prime location provides guests with panoramic views that seem almost unreal. Accessible via **Gustaf III Airport**, the hotel is just a short drive from the airport, making it convenient for international travelers. Once you arrive, you will immediately feel the warmth and beauty that awaits you both in the hotel and on the island itself.

## Accommodations

The hotel boasts 22 chic bungalows and villas, all inspired by traditional **West Indies architecture**. Each unit showcases a unique blend of eclectic and tropical design elements. From colorful textiles to elaborate headboards adorned with motifs of parrots and pineapples, every detail is meticulously curated to enhance the island experience.

### Room Features

Guests can choose between hillside villas with stunning vistas and beach-level accommodations right next to the iconic **Gyp Sea Beach Club**. The spacious rooms come with luxurious bathrooms that feature walk-in showers, along with charming wood and rattan accessories, which add a rustic touch to a modern design. For those seeking pure indulgence, larger villas are equipped with private pools, ensuring an exclusive experience.

## Dining Experiences

Dining at Gyp Sea is not just a meal; it’s an experience. The hotel restaurant specializes in **Mediterranean-inspired dishes** prepared with the freshest local ingredients. Enjoy your meals poolside under the dappled shade of palm trees or in the vibrant indoor lounge, which exudes colorful colonial charm.

### Chef’s Table and Cocktails

For a more exclusive experience, you can book a private Chef’s Table. Here, you can savor exquisitely crafted menus featuring premium ingredients like **lobster, truffles**, and exceptional **Wagyu beef**. After dinner, unwind at the Plantation Bar, where you’ll find a wide selection of rums alongside tropical-inspired cocktails, handcrafted to perfection.
