**IN BRIEF**

- **Air Caraïbes** will launch new service between **St. Maarten** and **St. Barthelemy**.

- Service scheduled to start on **12DEC25**.

- Flights operated by **Saint Barth Commuter** using **Cessna aircraft**.

- Coincides with resumption of **Paris Orly – St. Maarten** route.

- Flight details include departures from **SXM** and **SBH**.

Exciting news! **Air Caraïbes** is set to launch a brand new service connecting **St. Maarten** and **St. Barthelemy** starting in **December 2025**. This convenient route will be operated using **Saint Barth Commuter’s** Cessna aircraft, with the inaugural flight scheduled for **December 12, 2025**. This launch aligns perfectly with the resumption of the **Paris Orly – St. Maarten** route on the same day, making travel between these beautiful islands even more accessible. Get ready for a smooth and stylish journey!

## Flight Schedule and Details

Starting on **December 12, 2025**, the first flight will take off, exciting many who have awaited such connectivity. The schedule looks enticing for those planning their luxury escapes:

- **TX923:** Departure from SXM 16:45, Arrival at SBH 17:00
- **TX925:** Departure from SXM 17:00, Arrival at SBH 17:15
- **TX922:** Departure from SBH 16:00, Arrival at SXM 16:15
- **TX924:** Departure from SBH 16:15, Arrival at SXM 16:30

This easy-going schedule means quick getaways for those jet-setters looking to indulge in the finest experiences on both islands.
