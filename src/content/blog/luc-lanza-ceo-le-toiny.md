In a significant move that marks a new era for one of St. Barts' most luxurious establishments, **Luc Lanza** has officially taken over as the **CEO** of **Le Toiny**, succeeding the retiring **Charles Walker**. With three decades of experience on the island and a rich history in the hospitality industry, Lanza is set to lead the hotel through its latest transformation while maintaining its exclusivity and charm. Following a decade of dedicated service from Walker, the property is on the brink of exciting developments, with Lanza poised to leverage his expertise and vision for the future.

## The Legacy of Charles Walker

For the past ten years, Charles Walker has been the guiding force behind Le Toiny, leading the hotel through numerous successes and establishing it as one of the premier destinations in St. Barts. Under his stewardship, the hotel has not only maintained its **luxurious allure** but also underwent a full restoration that enhanced its reputation among high-profile guests and jet-setters alike. As Walker transitions into retirement, he will remain on the Board, ensuring that his wealth of knowledge continues to benefit the hotel through special projects.

Walker's decision to step down comes after a remarkable career in which he championed quality service and transformative experiences for guests. His legacy will serve as a vital foundation for Lanza as he embraces the CEO role, carrying forward the ethos of excellence that has become synonymous with Le Toiny.

## Luc Lanza: A Journey through Luxury Hospitality

Luc Lanza's journey is a compelling story woven into the very fabric of St. Barts' **luxury hospitality** scene. Arriving on the island at just 24 years old, he began his career working at a local restaurant in Gustavia, which laid the groundwork for what would become a remarkable ascent within the industry. His impressive trajectory saw him recruited by Eden Rock, where he served as the Food & Beverage Manager for 18 years, honing his skills and crafting experiences that delighted guests.

In 2015, Lanza was tapped by Charles and Mandie Vere Nicoll following their acquisition of Le Toiny. His vision and leadership helped to drive the hotel's transformation into a haven of **discreet luxury**, making it a sought-after escape for guests seeking tranquility and opulence. Lanza's intimate knowledge of the island and its unique offerings makes him an invaluable asset as the hotel moves forward under his guidance.
