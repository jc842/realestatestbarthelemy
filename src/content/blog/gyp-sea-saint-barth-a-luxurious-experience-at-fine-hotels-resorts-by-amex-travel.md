## Stunning Location and Accommodations

The location of **Gyp Sea Saint Barth** is simply unparalleled. Overlooking the dazzling **Bay of Flamands**, this hotel offers a serene escape for both vacationers and jet-setters. With only 22 bungalows and villas, guests are assured an exclusive experience that combines comfort with the beauty of the Caribbean.

These accommodations are inspired by traditional **West Indies architecture** and infused with a vibrant tropical design. Positioned either in the hills with breathtaking views or directly on **St Jean Beach** adjacent to their beach club, guests can choose their perfect slice of paradise. The rooms are adorned with eye-catching headboards and textiles featuring funky motifs like parrots and pineapples, creating a joyful atmosphere.

The bathrooms? Oh, they're just as charming! With modern walk-in showers complemented by chic wood and rattan details, every aspect of your stay is designed for your comfort. If you really want to up the ante, the larger villas come equipped with private pools where you can soak up the sun in complete privacy.
