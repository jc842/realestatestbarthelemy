The iconic **Marina St Barth** is taking a bold step by moving into a larger space on **Worth Avenue** in **Palm Beach**. This popular **resort wear boutique**, known for its upscale offerings from the chic island of **Saint Barthélemy**, is set to open its new location this fall. The new venue aims to enhance the shopping experience for sun-seekers and fashion enthusiasts alike, making **Marina St Barth** a must-visit destination.
