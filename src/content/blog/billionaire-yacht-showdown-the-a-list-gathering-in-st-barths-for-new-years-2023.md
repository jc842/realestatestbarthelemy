Known as a playground for celebrities and billionaire magnates, it's no wonder this quaint Caribbean enclave becomes the centre of attention every New Year.

This holiday season, the port of Gustavia presents a more relaxed vibe as the typical superyacht traffic features missing figures due to ongoing geopolitical events.
