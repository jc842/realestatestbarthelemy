## Embracing Fall with Open Arms

Each year, as the days grow shorter and the leaves begin to change, the excitement for fall festivities overtakes the community. The pumpkin patch at St. Bartholomew's has become a cherished tradition, symbolizing the arrival of cooler weather, cozy sweater season, and, of course, the TONS of pumpkins that fill the patch. This year's kickoff on **October 13th** was nothing short of spectacular, with a range of pumpkins stacked high, awaiting eager families.

## Behind the Scenes: The Journey of 5,000 Pumpkins

The journey to the patch starts well before opening day. A fleet of trucks delivers the succulent harvest right from the **Atlanta Farmer’s Market**. It’s a meticulous process organized by local businesses, including Darrell Griffis Trucking, ensuring the pumpkins arrive safely. A staggering **5,000 pumpkins** made the journey, and out of all those beauties, only three were deemed unfit for display! This speaks to the quality and selection that St. Bart's brings to the community.

## Something for Everyone: Varieties Galore

The variety found here is mesmerizing. From the classic orange round shapes to white and green varieties, there’s a pumpkin suited for every taste. In addition to pumpkins, visitors can also find **gourds** and **squash**, making it an all-encompassing autumn experience! Whether you’re looking for the perfect pumpkin to carve or just a quirky gourd to add to your seasonal decor, St. Bart’s has you covered.

## Family Fun: More Than Just a Pumpkin Patch

What truly sets this pumpkin patch apart is the sense of community and the activities that come along with it. The patch transforms into a festival landscape, offering more than just pumpkins. Family-friendly events fill the schedule, including story time, hayrides, and even the opportunity to learn about how pumpkins grow. Youngsters can engage with volunteers telling stories such as “Spookley the Square Pumpkin,” igniting their imaginations and teaching important lessons.

### Community Spirit in Full Swing

Volunteers play a pivotal role in the success of the pumpkin patch. Parishioners at St. Bartholomew's don’t just donate their pumpkins; they give their time and energy to create an inviting environment for the community. These volunteers help organize activities, assist families in selecting their pumpkins, and even create engaging storytelling moments for the kids. Each year, new faces join the crew, bringing fresh enthusiasm to this beloved event.

## Connecting with Nature: Educational Opportunities

One of the best ways to appreciate pumpkins (and all things fall) is by learning about them! Students from local schools participate in educational visits, where they learn about agriculture, environmental sustainability, and the science behind pumpkin growing. By integrating fun and education, children cultivate a deeper appreciation for nature and the seasonal changes around them.

## Timing is Everything: When to Visit the Patch

The pumpkin patch remains open through **October 28th** and may extend to **October 30th** if there are pumpkins left for eager visitors. So why wait? Grab your family, friends, or even that special someone and head down to the patch during its operational hours! Open Monday through Friday from 2-6 p.m., Saturday from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., there’s plenty of time to explore at your leisure.

## Supporting Local Causes

The pumpkin patch is more than just an autumnal fixture; it’s tied directly to the community's well-being. All profits from the pumpkin sales are funneled back into various outreach ministries including **Hope 4 Our Schools** and **Walking Tall Ministries**. So when you purchase a pumpkin, you’re not just getting a festive decoration for your home; you're supporting local initiatives that uplift and empower those around us.
