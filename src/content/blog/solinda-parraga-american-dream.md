Amidst the hustle and bustle of city life, a remarkable story unfolds at the 16th Street BART Plaza in San Francisco, where Solinda Parraga, a determined food vendor, transforms her dreams into reality. ## From Peru to the Promised Land

Solinda Parraga's adventure began far from the bright lights of San Francisco. Hailing from **Huancaya**, a quaint town in central Peru, she faced chains of physical and verbal abuse. After 15 tumultuous years, she made the life-altering decision to leave her abusive husband behind, escaping with dreams of a better life for herself and her children. The harsh rain that fell upon her during her early days in the USA mirrored the struggles she left behind, encapsulating the duality of her pursuit of the **American dream**.

### The Dangerous Escape

On December 21, 2023, she departed from her hometown with the guidance of her best friend, who had previously migrated to the U.S. Solinda transitioned through various cities, navigating immigration complexities, until she finally arrived in Arizona. Here, the **path would not be easy**; seven days in a detention center awaited her before she was moved to Texas. The entire experience was isolating, and the absence of communication from her friend added to the burden of her newfound freedom.

## Rediscovering Self-Worth

In the heart of Texas, as she was taken to the airport by immigration officials, hope flickered when a stranger, touched by her plight, purchased her ticket to San Francisco. This kind act opened a new chapter for Solinda. Settling in the city was not unchallenged, as she initially found herself sleeping outdoors, grappling with the reality of her circumstances.

### A Kindness Amidst Hardship

Despite the rigorous start, Solinda's strength prevailed. She was offered shelter by a fellow street vendor, showcasing the community spirit that exists within the toughest of situations. Gradually, she found a part-time job cleaning hotel rooms, which helped her secure a small apartment near the very plaza where she dreamed of starting her own food business.

## Starting a New Chapter at 16th St. BART Plaza

Today, Solinda proudly sells a variety of refreshing fruits at the bustling **16th St. BART Plaza**. Her vibrant stand, filled with luscious mangoes, melons, watermelons, oranges, and pineapples, creates a kaleidoscope of colors against the urban backdrop. This market has become a platform for her independence and an expression of her culture through food.

### The Daily Grind

Setting up her stand by 10 a.m. every day, Solinda’s work ethic shines. As the sun sets around her bustling stand, she interacts with customers, many of whom appreciate the flavors of her native Peru. The smiles from satisfied patrons reinforce her commitment to working hard and making a living, with every dollar offering her a chance to send money back home to her children. Some days are fruitful, while others barely cover her expenses, but her **resilient spirit** keeps her pushing forward.

## The Community Connection

For Solinda, it’s not just about sales; it’s about connections. She has cultivated friendships with surrounding vendors, creating a mosaic of cultural exchanges and stories at the plaza. From her left stands a smaller fruit cart that complements her offer, while a cozy hot dog cart to her right, managed by a young woman, adds to the communal feel.

### Neighborhood Challenges and Triumphs

With local police activity increasing and the neighborhood transforming, Solinda welcomes the presence of law enforcement, hoping it brings a safer environment for her customers and her business. As crime rates escalate and the community navigates the repercussions, her determination to remain a positive influence stands strong. She shares her thoughts on the increased police presence, expressing that while times are challenging, there is optimism in the fight against adversity.

## Facing the Future with Courage

As the December asylum hearing approaches, Solinda faces her fears and uncertainties about what lies ahead. The thought of returning to Peru sends shivers down her spine, as the specter of her abusive husband looms large. Yet, she remains undeterred, focusing on her immediate business and the opportunity of sending money back to her beloved children.

With every fruit sold at **16th St. BART Plaza**, she not only nurtures her own dreams but also becomes a symbol of hope for others in her community.

Leaving behind a toxic marriage was not an easy choice, yet, with the support of a friend, she embarked on this journey towards a better life. The emotional scars of her past lingered, but Parraga was determined to forge a new path. “He was a drunk. He beat me almost daily,” she recalls, reflecting on her turbulent past with a heavy heart.

After enduring a challenging journey that included **detention** in Arizona, Parraga’s luck changed when a kind stranger provided her with a ticket to San Francisco. “I felt like she abandoned me,” she admits about her friend, but the kindness of this stranger rekindled her hope. 

Parraga faced many hardships, including nights spent sleeping outdoors and relying on the generosity of others. Yet her resilient nature shone through as she began to carve out her niche in the bustling city. Selling fresh fruits like **mangoes**, **watermelons**, and **oranges** became her way of connecting with the community while building her dream. “Every day of the week, I set up a stand at the corner of 16th and Mission streets,” she shares with pride.

Although her sales fluctuate with the weather and crowd, Parraga’s determination remains unwavering. “Sometimes I can take a day off and send money home,” she reveals, emphasizing her devotion to supporting her family back in Peru.

Amid recent changes in police activity around the plaza, she feels a sense of security. “I feel good when the police are around,” she expresses while addressing the changing dynamics of the neighborhood.
