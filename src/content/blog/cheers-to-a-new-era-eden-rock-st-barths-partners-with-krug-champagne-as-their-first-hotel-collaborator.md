## The Luxury Destination Reinvented

Overlooking the stunning **St Jean Bay**, Eden Rock-St Barths has always been a dream destination for jet-setters and luxury seekers alike. This idyllic resort boasts **pristine white sandy beaches** and sparkling blue waters that draw visitors from all around the globe. With the advent of this new collaboration with Krug Champagne, the hotel is set to strengthen its reputation as a premier luxury destination.

The breathtaking views combined with the exquisite ambiance of Eden Rock create the perfect backdrop for savoring life’s finest moments. The reopening of the hotel for the new season carries a buzz of excitement, especially with the announcement of **Krug** as the new house champagne. Guests can now raise a toast with the finest bubbly as they indulge in the hotel's offerings.

## Unforgettable Experiences Await

As part of the new collaboration, every guest checking into Eden Rock-St Barths will be greeted with a complimentary bottle of **Krug Champagne** waiting in their room. Just imagine the thrill of popping open a bottle of Krug as you take in the stunning views of the Caribbean waters. It’s all about creating those unforgettable moments!

## A Culinary Delight with Krug

Indulge your palate at the various restaurants and bars spread across the stunning property. Not only can guests enjoy Krug Champagne in the comfort of their rooms, but they can also treat themselves to a curated selection of Krug Champagne across all dining venues. From the serene setting of **The Sand Bar**, where guests can savor a glass of **Krug Grande Cuvée** paired with renowned Chef **Jean-Georges Vongerichten’s** culinary masterpieces, to enjoying a glass of **Krug Rosé** on the sun deck of **The Eden Rock Beach Bar**, the options are endless.

Imagine indulging in delicious cuisine while sipping on the world’s finest champagne, all while overlooking the tranquil Caribbean Sea. The combination of taste, ambiance, and breathtaking views is what Eden Rock-St Barths aims to provide with this exquisite partnership.

## The Revelry of Private Tastings

For wine connoisseurs and those looking for a unique experience, the newly unveiled **Wine Cellar** in the Rémy Bar is the ultimate destination. As the richest wine cellar on the island, it offers a stylish space for private wine tastings led by the hotel’s expert sommeliers. This exquisite venue features an extensive collection of almost **1,000 references**, making it a paradise for wine lovers.

What’s more, these wines focus on sustainability and include organic and biodynamic options. Guests can enjoy a champagne sampling that includes both small producers and prestigious labels, ensuring a taste experience like no other.

## An Iconic Collaboration Rooted in Shared Values

The collaboration between Eden Rock-St Barths and Krug reflects their shared commitment to luxury, excellence, and creating unforgettable memories for their guests. *"We are thrilled to partner with Krug Champagne to bring an extra touch of magic to Eden Rock – St Barths,"* says **Fabrice Moizan**, General Manager of Eden Rock. This partnership aims to enhance the guest experience from the moment of arrival.

## Experience the Magic: How to Get There

As of September 2024, prices for a stay at Eden Rock-St Barths start at €4,500 per night, which includes breakfast and a bottle of Krug Champagne waiting in your room upon arrival. Guests can also take advantage of the captivating wine tastings in the Wine Cellar at Rémy Bar, which is available for groups of up to four. The prices for these exclusive tastings start from €175 per person, offering an engaging experience that combines education with delightful flavors.

Wine lovers can choose from several tasting options, each designed to introduce guests to an array of exquisite cuvées. It’s recommended to book tastings at least 48 hours in advance to ensure a slot for an experience that embodies the luxurious spirit of Eden Rock together with Krug Champagne.
