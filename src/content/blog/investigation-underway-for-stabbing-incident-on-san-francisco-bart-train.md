## Details of the Incident

Early on November 2nd, just after 8:00 a.m., a 54-year-old woman became the victim of a brutal stabbing while traveling on a BART train through the Mission District. Witnesses reported that the attack was sudden and without provocation. The assailant attacked the woman as they were nearing the **24th Street/Mission Station** and, following the chaos, a station agent promptly assisted the victim until BART police arrived on the scene.

### Immediate Response

Quick action from BART officials and station agents was crucial in this situation. After the assault, the woman was immediately taken to a local hospital, where she was treated for serious injuries. The police closures temporarily affected the operations at the 24th Street/Mission Station for about 45 minutes, creating a ripple of concern among passengers and commuters.

## Profile of the Suspect

Following the initial chaos of the attack, police identified the suspect as **Jovany Portades**, a 34-year-old man. Authorities were quick to release information to the public, encouraging anyone with knowledge about the incident to come forward. They described Portades as being pivotal to solving this crime, and his profile became a focal point of the ongoing investigation.

### Arrest of the Suspect

The next day, on Sunday afternoon, BART police successfully arrested Portades. The speedy capture provided some relief to anxious citizens who rely on public transportation daily. The incident also opened up discussions on the broader implications of safety and security on BART trains and other transit systems throughout San Francisco.
