### Who is Jeff Grant?

Jeff Grant is not just a lawyer; he is a renowned advocate for individuals affected by **white-collar crimes**. After facing his own tumultuous journey that saw him incarcerated for almost two years, Jeff transformed his life. He turned his experience into a mission, co-founding the **White Collar Support Group**, which assists those facing similar challenges. His transition from a lawyer, battling addiction and legal troubles, to a minister and counselor exemplifies the potential for **personal redemption**.

### The Importance of Criminal Expungement

Criminal expungement refers to the legal process that allows an individual to clear their criminal record after they have served their time, completed their parole or probation, and demonstrated good behavior. This process is crucial for many as it can open doors to employment, housing, and social reintegration. By discussing this topic, Jeff hopes to raise awareness about how expungement can significantly alter lives and provide a second chance to those who have made mistakes in the past.
