This Memorial Day weekend, the vibrant atmosphere of the Hamptons welcomed summer as **Leesa Rowland**, an actress, author, and philanthropist, hosted a fabulous celebration at **Marina St. Barth** in Southampton. Commemorating her feature on the cover of **MAT Magazine**, Rowland, the founder of the **Animal Ashram** nonprofit, highlighted a cause close to her heart, supporting animal welfare. Guests enjoyed exquisite offerings from **BIATCH Tequila** and **Wainscott Main Wine & Spirits**, featuring their signature rosé and refreshing summer cocktails. The event also showcased the stunning summer handbag collection by **Carole Alexander**, marrying classic craftsmanship with playful designs. Additionally, the celebration supported the **Hamptons Summer Songbook by-the-Sea**, a beloved musical initiative that dazzles the crowd with Broadway-caliber talent throughout the summer.

## Celebrating Summer with Leesa Rowland at Marina St. Barth

This past Memorial Day weekend, the Hamptons welcomed summer in style, all thanks to the incredible **Leesa Rowland**. Hosting an afternoon celebration at the chic **Marina St. Barth** in Southampton, her vibrant energy set the tone for a season filled with glamour and fun.

As the founder of **Animal Ashram**, a nonprofit dedicated to animal welfare, Leesa not only adds flair to social gatherings but also champions a cause close to her heart. The event was particularly special as it celebrated her cover feature in **MAT Magazine**, marking a milestone in her multifaceted career as an actress, author, and philanthropist.

Attendees enjoyed a delightful atmosphere filled with laughter, fashion, and refreshing sips from **BIATCH Tequila** and **Wainscott Main Wine & Spirits**, who provided their signature rosé and summer-inspired cocktails. The essence of St. Barth couldn't be missed, with the boutique's unique offering of clothing and accessories perfectly fit for both beachside brunches and glamorous evenings.

Furthermore, the event showcased the artistic talent of designer **Carole Alexander**, who presented her summer handbag collection under the label **Alexander by Design**. Each hand-adorned tote boasted a mix of classic craftsmanship and playful Amalfi-inspired styles, captivating attendees with their charm.

Adding another layer to the evening's festivities, the event supported the **Hamptons Summer Songbook by-the-Sea**, a praised musical initiative led by Donna Rubin and Josh Gladstone of LTV. This initiative promises to bring artistic excellence and Broadway-level talent to the East End through a series of extraordinary concerts, enriching the cultural landscape of the summer.
