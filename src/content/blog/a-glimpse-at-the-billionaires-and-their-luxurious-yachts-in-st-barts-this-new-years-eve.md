## Star-Studded Spectacle: Who's Anchoring in St. Barts

### Jeff Bezos and Lauren Sanchez

The waters off St. Barts have been graced by none other than **Jeff Bezos**, the Amazon founder, who has docked his stunning 410-foot yacht, *Koru*, just in time for the New Year's festivities. Accompanied by his girlfriend, Lauren Sanchez, Bezos has returned to his favorite island for the third consecutive year.

Reportedly valued at around $500 million, *Koru* was designed for ultimate luxury and can accommodate a select group of invited guests. With private bedrooms, an expansive deck, and a helipad, this majestic vessel is a sight to behold amidst the sparkling waters of Gustavia.

### Leonardo DiCaprio

**Leonardo DiCaprio** and his girlfriend Vittoria Ceretti have already been seen enjoying the sun-soaked beaches of St. Barts ahead of the New Year celebrations. Known for his love of sailing, Leo tends to anchor his luxury yacht nearby, allowing him to participate in the island's vibrant social scene.

### Eric Schmidt

The former CEO of Google, **Eric Schmidt**, has also made his presence known with his luxurious yacht, *Kismet*, docked in the bustling harbor. Although it remains uncertain if Schmidt is on board for the celebrations or if he has rented it out, this mega-boat boasts all the amenities one could ever desire, including a helipad and a swimming pool.

### Jerry Jones

Another familiar face on the waters of St. Barts is **Jerry Jones**, the billionaire owner of the Dallas Cowboys. His yacht, *Bravo Eugenia*, has also joined the fleet of impressive vessels anchored for the holiday, adding to the allure of the setting.

## Billionaires’ Yachts: Floating Paradises

### Nancy Walton

**Nancy Walton**, daughter of Walmart's founder, has been enjoying the holiday season aboard her extravagant yacht, *KAOS*. Known for its luxurious offering of 17 guest rooms, her ship draws considerable attention while stationed in the stunning harbor.

The Ukrainian-American WhatsApp co-founder, **Jan Koum**, has his spectacular mega yacht, *Mogambo*, docked at St. Barts as well. This impressive vessel, weighing more than 1,600 tons, is designed to accommodate 12 guests and 19 crew members, ensuring every comfort and amenity is at hand.
