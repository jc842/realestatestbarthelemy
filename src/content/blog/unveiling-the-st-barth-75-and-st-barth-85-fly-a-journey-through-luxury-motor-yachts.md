## The St-Barth 85 Fly: Expanding Horizons in Luxury

Set to take the yachting scene by storm in 2025, the **St-Barth 85 Fly** builds upon the foundation laid by its predecessor. This yacht is tailored for families and social gatherings, boasting an even larger design that perfectly fits the “mini superyacht” appeal.

### Expansive Outdoor Living

The St-Barth 85 Fly is defined by its luxurious flybridge, introducing expansive outdoor living. Featuring loungers, a shaded seating area for eight, a wine cooler, and an outdoor galley, this space embodies the ultimate relaxation. You can bask in the sun, take in the scenic views, or host a barbecue right on your yacht.

### Elevated Interior Experience

The interior of the St-Barth 85 Fly mirrors the St-Barth 75 but comes with upscale enhancements. A full-beam master suite located directly behind the saloon elevates the living experience, providing a sense of grandeur for owners and guests alike. This thoughtful design maximizes comfort and privacy for everyone on board.

### Community and Comfort Below Deck

Further along, the lower deck boasts three generously sized guest suites, each equipped with private bathrooms. This layout guarantees a cozy experience for everyone. For those wishing to travel with crew or maintain privacy, a dedicated crew area is available that can be transformed into additional guest accommodations.

### Adventure-Ready Design

At the back of the yacht, the beach club expands further, featuring a substantial four-meter-wide swim platform along with hidden tender storage. This is where adventure meets luxury, making it easy to embark on water sports adventures with family or friends. The innovative storage system provides ample space for a transparent adventure, encouraging long-lasting fun on sea expeditions.
