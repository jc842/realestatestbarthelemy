## The Importance of Supporting the Community

As the **housing crisis** continues to affect many in Western Australia, the need for community support has never been clearer. With an increasing demand for housing and a limited supply, vulnerable individuals are finding it harder to secure safe accommodations. This scenario is made even more challenging when paired with the **rising cost of living**, which puts additional pressure on those already struggling.

This is where **St Bart's** steps in. As a local charity, they offer support and accommodation services to those experiencing homelessness or facing mental health issues. Their dedication to rebuilding lives with dignity aligns perfectly with **Realmark's** values of community involvement and support. It's a collaboration that promises to make a real difference.

## Realmark's Commitment to Making a Difference

Realmark has been a pivotal player in the real estate market for over three decades. With a mission to not only succeed in the industry but also give back to the community that has supported them, Realmark is stepping forward this holiday season. They invite offices, agents, staff, and clients to participate in making this festive season meaningful for those who are less fortunate.

By engaging in fundraising efforts, Realmark aims to raise awareness and financial support for St Bart’s. As stated by **Anita Percudani**, co-founder of Realmark, “Partnering with St Bart's allows us to contribute to meaningful change by supporting individuals facing homelessness and mental health challenges.” This statement encapsulates the primary goal of their partnership: to foster a more **inclusive and connected community**.

## Festive Initiatives and Fundraising Events

This Christmas season, Realmark has planned several initiatives that focus on gathering both financial and physical support for St Bart's. They will be promoting their Christmas marketing campaign, encouraging various stakeholders to contribute in whatever way possible. This year, the focus will not only be on monetary donations but also on collecting essential items such as food and beverages.

Additionally, offices are set to organize local drives to gather donations, creating a buzz around the charity and highlighting the importance of sharing during the festive period. The collection of vital supplies is crucial to St Bart's Christmas drive, helping those who need it the most. With the public's help, Realmark aims to provide essential support during this challenging season.
