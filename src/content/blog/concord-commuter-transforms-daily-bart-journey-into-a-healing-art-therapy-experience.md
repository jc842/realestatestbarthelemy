## Transforming BART Rides into Artistic Journeys

A Concord commuter has turned her **BART** ride into a unique art therapy session. For nearly a decade, Joanna Sokua has been creating what she calls "**BARToons**," where she takes inspiration from fellow passengers and transforms them into hilarious pop-culture characters. During her 37-minute commute to work each day, she indulges in her passion for art, capturing the essence of those around her.

Sokua's artistic creations range from famous figures like **Vincent Van Gogh** to beloved cartoon characters. Her work not only serves as a creative outlet but also brings smiles and laughter to her subjects. By simply allowing her ideas to flow as she observes daily life, she embraces the diversity of **BART** riders and finds joy in her commuting routine.

In the bustling world of daily commutes, it’s easy to overlook the hidden talents of fellow passengers. One Concord resident has taken her daily BART ride into San Francisco and turned it into a therapeutic journey of creativity. For nearly a decade, Joanna Sokua has transformed her 37-minute rides into what she calls "BARToons" – lively illustrations that capture the personalities of the people she encounters and place them into imaginative pop-culture scenes. This article dives into her vibrant world of artistry, her inspiration, and the importance of art as a form of therapy in everyday life.

## Daily BART Adventures

Riding the Bay Area Rapid Transit system isn’t just about getting from point A to B; for Joanna, it's a daily chance for inspiration. “Taking BART is like stepping into a moving gallery where you meet a kaleidoscope of characters,” Joanna explains. Her commutes have morphed from a mundane part of the day into a captivating experience where surprises await at every corner. The variety of people she sees during her travels provides a constant flux of ideas that she channels into her art.

## Art as a Personal Outlet

Joanna has always had a passion for art, which blossomed from her childhood days. Despite the demanding nature of her day job in banking—helping small business owners navigate their challenges—art remains her sanctuary. She reflects, “It’s like my creative outlet—a break from that daily routine, you know?” Every time she boards the train, she carries her sketchpad and pencils as her tools of expression.

### The Art of Observing

Joanna’s artistic process is simple. She loves to people-watch and derive inspiration from the striking moments unfolding around her. She explained that something as small as a quirky hairstyle or a unique outfit can spark an idea that leads to an entire artwork. “I never know when an idea will pop into my mind,” Joanna says, “it just happens.” Her freedom to observe and draw whatever catches her eye allows her creativity to flourish.

## The Creation of BARToons

The magical part of Joanna's art is how she blends reality with the whimsical. Her "BARToons" feature her fellow commuters transformed into figures from their favorite movies and shows—like Scooby-Doo characters, superheroes, and famous artists. For Joanna, it’s a fun way to celebrate the unique qualities of individuals she encounters, while also offering a light-hearted take on daily commuting.

### Interacting with Commuters

Through social media, Joanna has created a vibrant community where she shares her creations. Each post often gets delightful reactions from those she sketches. Many commuters are thrilled to see themselves rendered into art, often commenting on how this brings joy to their day. One viewer called her art “so much fun” and encouraged her to keep capturing moments on BART. The connections Joanna creates through her art are heartwarming; it brings a smile and sparks conversations, reminding everyone to find joy in the small moments.

## Art Therapy: Healing Through Creativity

Joanna's journey goes beyond her enjoyment of drawing; it speaks to the essence of art therapy. The concept of art as a form of healing has gained popularity, with many acknowledging its positive impact on mental health. Joanna embodies this idea—her BART rides become a platform for both personal and communal healing through collective laughter and intrigue. “If I can make someone laugh or smile for just a minute or two, I’ll keep doing it,” she shares.

### Why Art Therapy Matters

Art therapy works as a bridge between the mind and the creative world. It enables people to express thoughts and feelings that might be hard to verbalize. Joanna emphasizes how her commute creates a space for that expression, helping her unwind from the stress of daily work life. It’s not just about drawing; it’s about releasing energy, sharing joy, and connecting with a broader community. The power of art lies in its ability to heal, making it an essential tool for many.

## A Glimpse into the Future

As Joanna continues to ride the BART and create her beloved BARToons, she is hopeful and excited for what lies ahead. She dreams of expanding her art beyond the confines of her sketchpad—perhaps turning her little artworks into a collection or even merchandising her illustrations. “I hope to keep doing this for years to come, and who knows what else I may accomplish?” she muses. Her commitment to her art is evident, and she is eager to see how it can evolve and connect with even more people.

Meet Joanna, a vibrant woman who has turned her daily BART ride into a canvas for her imagination. For nearly a decade, she has been sketching "BARToons," capturing the unique personalities of her fellow commuters and placing them into entertaining, pop-culture scenes. What started as a simple hobby has become her go-to **art therapy** session during the daily grind.

“Every day I hop on BART, it’s like a new adventure waiting to unfold,” Joanna shares, her eyes lighting up with excitement. “I pull out my sketchbook, and just like that, the 37-minute ride transforms into my personal creative studio!”

What makes her artistry special is the organic inspiration she draws from people-watching. “You would be surprised by the characters you meet,” she says. “Some folks are so unusual, they practically jump off the page! It’s amazing how much life you can find on a single train journey.”

Joanna often finds herself illustrating fellow commuters as superheroes, historical figures, and well-loved animated characters. “I like to think my art gives them a fun twist!” she laughs, reminiscing about the time a commuter excitedly recognized himself as a Scooby Doo character on her Instagram.

“I’m just trying to add a spark to someone’s day,” Joanna says. “If I can take your average commute and turn it into a laugh or smile, then why not? We all need a little joy in our lives.”

Through her art, Joanna not only embraces her creativity but also promotes a sense of community among the diverse ridership on BART. “Art has a magic way of connecting us. We come from different places and backgrounds, but art bridges those gaps,” she expresses passionately.

Each day, Joanna’s sketches offer her a much-needed break from her banking job. “Crunching numbers all day can be intense!" she admits. “But when I draw, it’s like I’m in my own world. It’s my way to unwind and recharge.”

As she continues her artistic journey, Joanna hopes to inspire others to find their own outlets for creativity and joy during their daily routines. “Who knows? Maybe my little sketches could spark someone else’s creativity too!”
