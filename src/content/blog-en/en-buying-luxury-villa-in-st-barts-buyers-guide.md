# Acquiring a Luxury Villa in St. Barts: Private Wealth Advisory & Due Diligence

Saint-Barthélemy represents one of the most stable and exclusive real estate micro-markets globally, characterized by limited inventory, strict building density codes, and global ultra-high-net-worth demand.

## The Legal Purchasing Framework

Transactions are conducted under French civil notary oversight, ensuring undisputed registered ownership rights.

### Key Acquisition Steps
1. **Preliminary Contract (*Compromis de Vente*)**: Locks purchase price, inventory of bespoke furnishings, and conditions precedent.
2. **Escrow Deposit**: Standard 10% deposited into official French Notary escrow.
3. **Registration Duties & Collectivity Transfer Tax**: Approximately 5% local registration duty (*droit d'enregistrement*) payable to the Collectivité of Saint-Barthélemy.

Private estates situated on hillsides overlooking Saline, Governeur, or Colombier retain resilient liquidity across economic cycles.
